Epsilon Chop

Drop in a sample. Epsilon Chop cuts it into colour-coded chops, lays them on a 16-step ribbon, and flips them with one roll of the dice. This manual covers every control, shows where each one lives, and explains what happens inside.

CHAPTER 01

Five clicks to your first flip

  1. Drop a sample anywhere on the window, or click LOAD SAMPLE. WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 and OGG, up to 120 seconds.

  2. Keep TRANSIENT for drums and vocals, or pick GRID → 16 for a clean loop.

  3. Click IN ORDER, then PLAY. You hear the loop rebuilt from its own chops. FL Studio doesn't need to be playing.

  4. Press RANDOMIZE. Chops trade places, and the readout under the button tells you which ones, for example Swapped 11 ↔ 13.

  5. Keep the result, or click UNDO and roll again. Save the ones you like to banks B, C and D.

The header

Loading never interrupts audio.

Decoding and analysis run on a background thread, and the new sample crossfades in over 2 ms. Your four banks are kept. Undo history clears, and LOOP BPM goes back to auto-detect.

CHAPTER 06

Randomize: flip it fast

Toggles choose what may change. CHAOS chooses how much. Every roll can be undone, and if nothing is playing, a roll starts PLAY so you hear it at once.

SWAP: chops trade places

A swap of 11 and 13 turns every 11 into 13 and every 13 into 11. Timing, gates, ratchets, pitch and reverse stay put. The partner can be any chop in the sample, so swaps also pull in parts of the sample that weren't in the bank yet. Number of swaps = CHAOS × (different chops in the bank) ÷ 2. At 95% and above, every chop is shuffled at once.

The other five toggles

RHYTHM

Moves CHAOS × hits to new steps, favouring downbeats (1, then 9, 5, 13). Sometimes it adds or drops a hit, keeping 4–13 hits and never removing step 1.

STUTTER

Ratchets of 2, 3 or 4 repeats inside a step. Each hit has a CHAOS × 32% chance. Repeats play at 82% level.

REVERSE

Plays chops backwards. Each hit has a CHAOS × 30% chance.

PITCH

Shifts hits by −12, −7, −5, +5, +7 or +12 semitones. Each hit has a CHAOS × 30% chance.

GATE

Sets hit lengths to 25, 40, 60, 80 or 100%. Each hit has a CHAOS chance.

CHAOS, per hit

How likely each hit is to change at a given CHAOS setting.

Good to know

With STUTTER, REVERSE or PITCH on, every hit is re-rolled for that property, so turn a toggle off to keep what you set by hand. An empty bank gets a new 5–9 hit phrase. A roll that would change nothing forces one swap. Bank switching, the toggles and CHAOS are settings, so UNDO leaves them alone.

CHAPTER 08

MIDI & the Piano Roll

Every key plays a chop. C5 is where FL's step sequencer plays, so a new channel makes sound straight away.

With 8 chops: C5 → Chop 01, each key up is the next chop, G#5 wraps to Chop 01 again, and B4 plays Chop 08. Set the root in the footer, from C1 to C7. Velocity sets the level, and CC 120/123 silences notes.

Mono choke

A new hit fades the previous one out over 2 ms: classic MPC chopping. MIDI hits play the chop plainly. Step pitch, reverse, ratchet and gate belong to the ribbon.

CHAPTER 09

Under the hood

From audio file to audio out.

BACKGROUNDDecode64k-frame blocks at the file's own rate. Bad values are zeroed.
BACKGROUNDOnsetsSpectral flux with a 3-bin max filter. Up to 256 hits, kept with their strengths.
INSTANTCutsThe mode picks from the stored onsets, then ZERO-X snaps each cut.
LOCK-FREESessionAn immutable snapshot is published to the audio thread.
AUDIOClockHost PPQ or the internal clock. Sample-exact steps, swing and ratchets.
AUDIOVoicesMono choke, 8 voices, 2 ms fades. 32-tap sinc for pitch and rate.

Finding the hits

A Hann window of at least 16 ms hops a quarter window at a time. The log magnitude of the louder channel is taken per bin. Novelty is each bin's rise above the maximum of its three neighbours one frame earlier, which ignores vibrato and slides (Böck & Widmer, DAFx 2013). A frame becomes a candidate at a local peak that clears 1.25 × the mean of ±10 frames. The cut then slides back to where the waveform first reaches 15% of its local peak, minus 1 ms. Hits closer than 35 ms to a stronger one, or within 10 ms of either end, are dropped.

Choosing the cuts

Transient keeps onsets whose strength is at least strongest × (0.6 × (1 − sensitivity/100)² + 0.004), and the strongest ones win when there are more than MAX allows. That's why the slider responds instantly. Beat divides by LOOP BPM and pulls each cut onto a hit within 20% of a division or 40 ms. Random spaces cuts at least length ÷ (4 × count) apart. Automatic chops are at least min(25 ms, length/64) long.

Loop BPM

The loop is assumed to be a power-of-two number of 4/4 beats (1, 2, 4 … 256), and the length whose tempo lands in 80–160 BPM is chosen. A 4-second file reads as 120 BPM (8 beats). If it's off by double or half, drag LOOP BPM.

Clock

Host: step = FL's quarter-note position × steps per quarter, looping from bar 1. A jump in FL (loop, click, tempo change) re-anchors without a stray hit. Internal: starts at step 1 on PLAY. Output is identical at any buffer size (tested at 64, 257 and 1,024 frames).

Sound

A 32-tap Blackman-windowed sinc with 64 sub-sample phases handles a different sample rate, step pitch and Tempo Fit. Reading faster than 1× switches to one of 81 narrower filters to stop aliasing. At matching rates with no pitch, samples are read directly with no filtering at all. Every voice fades in and out over 2 ms, and output is smoothed over 8 ms.

Real-time safety & CPU

The audio thread never allocates, locks or reads files. Edits publish a new snapshot, guarded by a hazard pointer. The editor polls at 30 Hz. The waveform and ribbon are cached images, so during playback only the playhead and step outline redraw. Debug and Release are both -O3. An unoptimised build says so in the footer.

Saved with your project

The decoded sample (so projects open even if the file moved), the mode and cuts, all four banks, randomizer settings and seed, and every parameter. That's about 23 MB per minute of 48 kHz stereo. A check marker rejects damaged data, and v0.1.0 sessions still open.

CHAPTER 10

Recipes & fixes

Flip a loop, keep the groove

GRID → 16 (or BEAT → 1/16), then IN ORDER. Turn on TEMPO FIT if tempos differ. Leave only SWAP on, set CHAOS to 20–35%, and roll until a swap catches your ear. The readout tells you what moved.

Chop a drum break

TRANSIENT at sensitivity 60–75, MAX 16. SWAP + RHYTHM + STUTTER at 50%. RATE 1/16, SWING 15–25%.

Vocal chops

TRANSIENT at low sensitivity, or RANDOM → 8. SWAP + PITCH + REVERSE at 40%. Pull gates down for stabs.

SymptomFix
No soundPress PLAY, or turn on HOST SYNC and start FL. Check the bank isn't all rests, then check OUTPUT and the mixer track.
